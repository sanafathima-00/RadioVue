# Recon Specification

## Purpose

Investigate CT-to-X-ray projection as a differentiable or otherwise measurable consistency-validation mechanism.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Geometry and comparison

Each Recon experiment SHALL document projection geometry, preprocessing, compared views, and comparison metrics.

### Requirement: Validation role

Recon outputs SHALL be treated as consistency evidence; they do not by themselves establish clinical validity of a generated CT.
